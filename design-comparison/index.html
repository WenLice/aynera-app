App direction · current vs proposed

A clearer Aynera, screen by screen.

A standalone comparison of the strongest ideas from the Aynera website translated into mobile. The left side preserves the current app structure. The right side shows the recommended direction.

Four focused improvements
  • Make Track a real product choice
  • Separate Duos and Squads inside Meet
  • Move safety from settings into the journey
  • Show the four chapters without lengthening registration

PAGE 01 · REGISTRATION

Choose your Track

The current page asks what a connection could become, but all answers sit in one flat list. The proposed version first distinguishes Fluid from Intent, then lets the member name a clearer path. This creates Aynera-owned vocabulary and makes future matching easier to explain.

PAGE 02 · MEET

Duos and Squads

The app already has one-to-one introductions and gatherings, but they appear as unrelated content. Naming them Duos and Squads turns both into deliberate ways of meeting inside one local network.

PAGE 03 · MEET PLANNING

Safety in context

The current plan screen includes useful controls, but it does not frame them as Aynera’s safety promise. The proposal brings verification, public-place guidance, optional check-ins, and trusted-person sharing into one calm safety layer.
